# Administrator roles

Tickminder distinguishes the platform owner, each organisation's chief administrator, and ordinary administrators.

| Role | Administrator account management |
| --- | --- |
| Platform owner | Select an organisation, appoint or replace its chief, and manage its ordinary administrators. |
| Organisation chief administrator | Create, activate, suspend, restore and reset passwords for ordinary administrators in that organisation. |
| Ordinary administrator | Retain existing teaching and student administration access; cannot create administrators or manage another administrator's identity or credentials. |

## Appointing and replacing a chief

The platform owner chooses an active administrator in **Platform console → organisation → Group chief administrator**. Review the selected person and confirm the appointment or handover. Existing accounts are never automatically promoted.

An organisation with no chief displays **Awaiting appointment**. Once a chief is appointed, replacement is a single handover: the previous chief remains an ordinary administrator and is signed out. Appoint a successor before suspending, demoting or permanently removing the current chief. A stale page cannot overwrite another completed handover.

## Accounts and sign-in

**People & accounts → Accounts** identifies the chief separately from ordinary administrators. Permitted account managers can create a username and temporary password. The new user must change the temporary password before accessing business features. A password reset signs out the affected account and requires another password change. The platform owner can reset a chief's password for account recovery.

Usernames remain unique across the platform. Google sign-in and existing account-linking rules continue to apply. Passwords and password hashes are never shown in account lists or action history.

Administrator identity changes are recorded in activity history. Organisation boundaries and existing protections for the platform owner remain in effect. This release adds administrator hierarchy; module-by-module permissions are a separate feature. Billing remains limited to the platform owner's current pilot.
